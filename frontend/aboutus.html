MANDALAY CORE

Established in 2026, NaNb — short for No Aim No Brain — was founded by a collective of streetwear enthusiasts dedicated to bringing global sneaker culture and highly coveted hype drops closer to local collectors.

Based in Mandalay, we bridge the gap between premium international brands and Myanmar's growing community of hypebeasts, resellers, and first-time buyers who want verified pieces without the guesswork.

How We Work

THE NANB AUTHENTICATION FLOW

Every product passes through the same inspection pipeline — whether it's a grail sneaker or a local brand tee.

01
Source & Intake

Items are sourced from trusted suppliers and local partners, then logged into our vault with photos and serial details.

02
Legit Check

Our curators inspect stitching, tags, box labels, materials, and sole patterns against reference standards.

03
Grade & List

Verified pieces receive an appraisal grade, accurate sizing data, and go live on the marketplace with full transparency.

04
Ship & Track

Once sold, orders move through Pending → Authenticating → In Transit → Delivered with a trackable vault ID.

Since Day One

OUR ROADMAP

From a small Mandalay resale circle to a full streetwear marketplace — here's how NaNb has been building the culture.

2024
The Spark

A group of Mandalay collectors starts sourcing authentic sneakers for friends and local hypebeasts — word spreads fast through campus and streetwear circles.

Q1 2025
First Pop-Up Hub

NaNb launches its first legit-check pop-up and social storefront, offering physical inspection and same-week local delivery in Mandalay.

Q3 2025
Local Brand Wave

Partnerships with 10+ Myanmar streetwear labels go live — giving rising designers a verified platform alongside global grail drops.

Jan 2026
NaNb Goes Official

No Aim No Brain registers as NaNb, opens the Mandalay warehouse, and standardizes authentication grading for every item.

Q2 2026
Marketplace Launch

Full online store goes live with cart, wishlist, order tracking radar, AI bargaining, and account-based purchase history.

2026+
What's Next

Nationwide delivery expansion, collector meet-ups, drop calendars, resale index tools, and more local designer collaborations across Myanmar.
